You use two timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, in which case you want to fail quickly after a short connect timeout, or it may be reachable but slow while generating, so you allow a longer total timeout for the answer to complete [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With streaming, that second timeout can instead bound idle gaps between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
