You set two different timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes that need different limits: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to fail fast after a second or two, while it may also be reachable but slow to generate, where waiting longer is worthwhile [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

In practice, `connect_timeout` handles “can’t reach them,” and `timeout` bounds how long you’re willing to wait for the answer to finish generating [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With streaming, that second timeout changes meaning because the connection stays open while tokens arrive, so it effectively bounds idle gaps between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
