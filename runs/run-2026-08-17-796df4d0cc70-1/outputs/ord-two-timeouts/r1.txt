You set two timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to fail quickly; or it may be reachable but still generating slowly, so you’re willing to wait longer for a real answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. A single timeout can’t separate “can’t reach them” from “reachable but slow,” whereas `connect_timeout` handles the outage tripwire and `timeout` bounds how long you’ll wait for generation [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
