An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but whose vectors are still in the index—and can still win retrieval for some query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause stale guidance to surface in answers: a user might act on outdated policy, hours, or procedures without realizing the information is obsolete [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. If your indexing pipeline only adds new vectors and never deletes old ones, those orphaned fragments can persist indefinitely [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

That’s why the docs recommend deleting a document’s old vectors in the same operation that writes its new ones, stamping chunks with last-indexed timestamps, and running a reconciliation job to remove chunks whose source documents no longer exist [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
