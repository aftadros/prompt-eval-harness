An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten earlier but is still left in the index and can “win retrieval” for a later query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks make an assistant return stale guidance: users may act on outdated information, and answers can rot without anyone noticing until it becomes a real problem [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. They are especially dangerous if your indexing pipeline only ever adds new vectors instead of deleting old ones; in that case, every superseded chunk stays around forever [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. That’s why the docs recommend deleting a document’s old vectors when it changes and running reconciliation to remove chunks whose source no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
