An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors remain in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because an orphaned chunk can still be retrieved and “win” for some query, causing the assistant to return outdated guidance from a version of the document that no longer applies [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. In practice, this is why deletes matter as much as writes: when a document changes, you should remove its old vectors in the same operation that writes the new ones so retrieval doesn’t straddle two versions [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. Orphaned chunks can also creep in even if your pipeline normally behaves, which is why it’s worth running a reconciliation job to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
