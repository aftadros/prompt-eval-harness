Because an LLM call has two very different failure modes, and a single timeout can’t handle both well: the provider may be unreachable, in which case you want to fail quickly after a second or two; or it may be reachable but slow to generate, in which case you’re willing to wait longer because a real answer is coming. Two timeouts let you use a short connection timeout as an “outage tripwire” while allowing a longer total timeout for generation [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
