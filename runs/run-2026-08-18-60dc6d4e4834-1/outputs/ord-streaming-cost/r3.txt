No—not directly. Streaming doesn’t make the model call cheaper or faster: you still pay for the same output tokens and the model spends the same time producing them [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].

It can reduce cost indirectly, though, because streaming improves perceived latency and reduces retries caused by users waiting on a blank screen; each retry would otherwise pay for another generation of the answer [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
