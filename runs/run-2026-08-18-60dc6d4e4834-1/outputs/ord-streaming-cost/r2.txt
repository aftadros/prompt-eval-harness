No—not directly. Streaming does not make the model cheaper or faster: you pay for the same output tokens and the model still spends the same time producing them [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].

It can reduce total cost indirectly, though. By lowering perceived latency and avoiding blank-screen waits, streaming reduces user retries; it also changes timeout behavior because a visibly progressing answer can keep its connection open instead of failing and becoming another paid retry [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries]. In practice, you then bound the idle gap between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
