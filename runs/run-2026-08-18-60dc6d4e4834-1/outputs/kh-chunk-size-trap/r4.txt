He doesn’t recommend a specific numeric chunk size. Instead, he recommends chunking along the document’s own structure first—splitting on headings, keeping list items with their parent and tables with captions—and using a token limit only as a fallback when a single section is genuinely too long [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find]. The 512-token figure appears only as an example of fixed-window chunking, not as his recommendation.
