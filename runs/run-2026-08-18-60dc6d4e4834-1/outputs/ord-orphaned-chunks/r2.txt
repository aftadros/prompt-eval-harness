An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of an old version of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but its vectors are still sitting in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they can keep winning retrieval for queries and cause users to act on stale guidance. A pipeline that only adds new chunks without deleting old ones will retain these orphaned fragments forever, so deletes matter just as much as writes [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
