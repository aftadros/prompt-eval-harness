An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten months ago, but is still sitting in the index and can keep winning retrieval for some unlucky query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they are described as “the classic production failure” in RAG systems: if your pipeline only ever adds vectors and never deletes old ones, it keeps those stale fragments forever, causing users to act on outdated guidance [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. The fix is to delete a document’s old vectors in the same operation that writes its new ones, ideally alongside reconciliation jobs that remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
