Phrase each LLM-judge prompt as **one narrow yes-or-no question**, with the evaluation criterion spelled out explicitly. Include:

- The source text to be checked against  
- An instruction to verify claims against that provided text, not the judge model’s prior beliefs  
- A requirement for the verdict first, followed by explanation  

Avoid asking for a bare 1–10 score, since that tends to produce vague scores clustered around 7 [evals-before-vibes--llm-as-judge-and-where-it-lies-to-you].
