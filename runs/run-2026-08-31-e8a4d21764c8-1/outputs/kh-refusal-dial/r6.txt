No—not always. The documents describe refusal as a tradeoff: if the assistant is too cautious it will “shrug at questions it could have answered,” while if it is too eager to answer it may hallucinate [rag-over-internal-docs--the-honest-i-dont-know].

The guidance is to refuse when the retrieval results are weak or thin, and to lean toward refusal for decision-critical documentation where a wrong answer costs more than a missed one [rag-over-internal-docs--the-honest-i-dont-know]. But it is not an instruction to refuse every uncertain question.
