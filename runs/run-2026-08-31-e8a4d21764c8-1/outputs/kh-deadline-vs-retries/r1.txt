You missed a deadline for the **entire retried operation**, not just each individual call. The documents point out that retries multiply the worst-case wait: three attempts against a 30-second timeout can mean about ninety seconds plus backoff before your fallback fires [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation].

The fix is to add an overall operation deadline separate from the per-call timeouts, and when that deadline passes, stop retrying and degrade or fall back instead of continuing to wait [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation].
