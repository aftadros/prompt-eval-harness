You missed an **overall operation deadline** for the retried call. Retries can multiply your per-call timeout: three attempts with a 30-second ceiling can become up to ~90 seconds plus backoff before you fall back [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation].

The fix is to keep timeouts separate—e.g., a fast `connect_timeout` for unreachable providers and a longer total timeout for slow generation—and then wrap the whole retry loop in its own deadline. When that overall budget expires, stop retrying and degrade [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts], [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation].
