No. Streaming does not make the model call itself cheaper: you still pay for the same output tokens, and the model spends the same time producing them [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].

It can indirectly help control costs by reducing perceived latency-related retries—users are less likely to resubmit when they see progress instead of a blank screen—and by changing timeout behavior so slow-but-progressing answers don’t get failed and re-run [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
