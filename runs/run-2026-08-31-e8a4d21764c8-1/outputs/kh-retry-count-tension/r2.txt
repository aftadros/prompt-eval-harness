They recommend a small, bounded retry budget—specifically about **two retries** for a model call. One post explicitly says retries are “usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger], and the other’s PHP example uses a hard cap of `$attempt >= 3` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry]. In practical terms, that means no more than roughly **three total attempts** rather than unlimited retries.
