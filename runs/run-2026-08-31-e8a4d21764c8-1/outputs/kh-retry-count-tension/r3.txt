They don’t give one universal number: one post’s PHP retry example hard-caps at `3` by returning false when `$attempt >= 3` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry], while the other says retries are “usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
