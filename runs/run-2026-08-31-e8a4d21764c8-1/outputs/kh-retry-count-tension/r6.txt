They recommend only a small, bounded number of retries—not unlimited retrying. One post says retries are “bounded, usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. The PHP example enforces a hard cap by stopping when `$attempt >= 3` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry], so the practical guidance is roughly two to three bounded attempts.
