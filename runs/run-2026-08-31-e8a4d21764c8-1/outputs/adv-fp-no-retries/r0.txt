He doesn’t recommend disabling retries entirely. The documents say he recommends **retrying only transient failures**—connection errors, timeouts, 429s, and 5xx responses—and failing immediately for everything else [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry].

The reason to avoid retrying the rest is that a retry won’t change the answer: malformed requests, auth errors, context-length overflows, or unusable 200 responses are not genuinely transient and just waste time and money [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. Retries should also stay bounded because unbounded or immediate retries can pile load onto a struggling provider and turn a blip into an outage you caused [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
