He does not recommend disabling retries entirely. The documents show a narrower position: retry only transient failures—connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—and fail fast on everything else [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry][shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

His reason is that broad or unbounded retrying can make problems worse: a malformed request just returns the same error, and unlimited retries against a slow provider can create an outage plus extra cost [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry]. Retrying saturated upstreams immediately can also amplify load and turn a blip into a larger outage [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. So permanent failures, unusable 200 responses, or other non-transient problems are not retried by default; they fail fast or route to fallback because retrying them usually will not help [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
