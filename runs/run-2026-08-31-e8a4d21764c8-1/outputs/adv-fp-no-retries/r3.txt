He doesn’t recommend disabling retries entirely. The documents say he recommends **retrying only transient failures**—such as connection errors, timeouts, `429` rate limits, and `5xx` responses—while letting other failures fail immediately [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry][shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

The reason is that retrying non-transient problems is wasteful or harmful: a malformed `400`, auth error, or context-length overflow won’t be fixed by another attempt [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger][llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry]. Likewise, retries should be bounded and back off so they don’t add load to an already struggling upstream service [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger][llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry].
