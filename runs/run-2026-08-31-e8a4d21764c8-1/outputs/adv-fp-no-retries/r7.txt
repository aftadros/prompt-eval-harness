He doesn’t recommend disabling retries entirely. The documents say to retry **only transient failures**—such as connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—and to fail fast on permanent problems like malformed requests or auth errors [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

Retries should be **bounded**, with exponential backoff and jitter, so they don’t add more load to an already struggling service [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
