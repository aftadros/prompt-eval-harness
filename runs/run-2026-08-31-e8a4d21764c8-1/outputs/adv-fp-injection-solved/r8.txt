None of the techniques described here fully solves prompt injection. The documents say it remains “a real, open problem” and that “no single one has to hold” because you rely on multiple layers [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth]. Delimited retrieved context and provenance framing only shift the base rate; they are not immunity [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary] and lower success rates without being a guarantee [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output]. Escaping outputs, limiting tool permissions, and keeping retrieval away from outbound actions reduce impact, but the goal is narrower: “keeping one planted instruction from being fatal,” not making the model safe against arbitrary text [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
