Give every model call an explicit timeout rather than relying on SDK defaults [shipping-llm-features-safely--timeouts-and-where-the-number-comes-from]. Decide it by working backward from the user-facing deadline: start with how long a human will wait and the server’s connection ceiling, then subtract time needed for retrieval, guardrails, serialization, and other non-model work [shipping-llm-features-safely--timeouts-and-where-the-number-comes-from].

For streaming calls, use two checks: total wall time and token flow. Track time-to-first-token and inter-token gaps so you can distinguish a long but healthy response from a stalled connection; the source says five seconds of nothing on the wire is a stall to kill [shipping-llm-features-safely--timeouts-and-where-the-number-comes-from]. If you need internal-service timeout defaults, they are listed in the platform wiki [wiki-page-timeouts].
