You set two timeouts because an LLM API call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, or it may be reachable but slow to finish generating [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. A short **connect timeout** lets you fail fast on an outage in a second or two, while a longer overall timeout lets you wait for a real answer that is still being produced [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

For example, `connect_timeout => 2` acts as the “outage tripwire,” and `timeout => 30` is the total ceiling for a non-streaming call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With streaming, the second timeout can instead bound the idle gap between chunks rather than the entire request, which helps keep visibly progressing responses from being failed into paid retries [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts], [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
