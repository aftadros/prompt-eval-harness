You set two different timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, in which case you want to fail quickly; or it may be reachable but slow while generating a real answer, in which case you’re willing to wait longer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

In practice, that means using a short connect timeout as an outage tripwire and a longer total timeout for generation time. With streaming, the distinction matters even more: the connection stays open while tokens arrive, so you may bound the idle gap between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. That helps avoid failing a slow-but-progressing answer and turning it into an extra paid retry [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
