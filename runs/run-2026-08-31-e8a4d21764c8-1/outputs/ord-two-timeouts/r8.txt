You set two timeouts because an LLM API call has two different failure modes that need different handling [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. One is that the provider is unreachable, where you want to fail quickly in a second or two. The other is that the provider is reachable but slow to finish generating, where you may be willing to wait longer for a real answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

In practice, `connect_timeout` acts as an outage tripwire, while `timeout` sets the total ceiling for a non-streaming call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. For streaming calls, the connection stays open while tokens arrive, so you typically bound the idle gap between chunks instead of the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
