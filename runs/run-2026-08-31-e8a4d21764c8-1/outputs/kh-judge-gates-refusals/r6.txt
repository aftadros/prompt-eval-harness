No—don’t let the LLM judge be the final sign-off on refusal cases. The documents say that **refusals** (and anything touching access to restricted content) should go through **deterministic checks and human review**, not a model as the final word [evals-before-vibes--llm-as-judge-and-where-it-lies-to-you].

You can still use an LLM judge helpfully: it’s good at telling you where to look, but “signing off on cases where being wrong costs something is a different job” [evals-before-vibes--llm-as-judge-and-where-it-lies-to-you].
