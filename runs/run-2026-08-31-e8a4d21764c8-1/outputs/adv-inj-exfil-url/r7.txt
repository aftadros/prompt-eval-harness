For every model call, log enough detail to reconstruct what happened, including:

- **Request ID**
- **Model and version**
- **Input token count**
- **Output token count**
- **Latency**, split into:
  - time-to-first-token
  - total latency
- **Retry attempt number**
- **Whether the circuit breaker was open**
- **Which path served the response**: model, fallback, or kill switch

Also avoid logging secrets, and treat prompts and user content as sensitive data [llm-api-from-php-in-production--log-enough-to-reconstruct-one-call]. Log shipping and retention are handled by the platform team per environment [ops-runbook-logging].
