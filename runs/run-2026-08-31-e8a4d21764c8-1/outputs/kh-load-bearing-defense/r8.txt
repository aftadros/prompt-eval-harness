The single most important defense is **enforcing permissions at retrieval time**: filter candidate documents against the user’s access rights before they are ranked or assembled into the prompt, so restricted material never enters the context window [prompt-injection-in-rag--push-the-defense-earlier-than-the-prompt].

That permission filter is described as the “load-bearing piece,” because even a perfectly planted instruction in an indexed document still cannot reveal data the user was not cleared to access; everything else—prompt framing, output escaping, tool scoping, and screening for attack patterns—mostly raises the attacker’s cost [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
