No. Retrieved chunks from different sources should not be flattened into one undifferentiated block of prompt context; their provenance matters, and the system prompt/context should reflect differences in trust [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--retrieved-content-is-hostile-input]. A chunk from a curated, write-restricted knowledge base deserves more trust than one from user-uploaded files, third-party indexed content, or editable wiki pages [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--retrieved-content-is-hostile-input].

The documents also recommend handling this before the prompt where possible: enforce access permissions at retrieval time and keep provenance attached so lower-trust sources can be treated with more suspicion [prompt-injection-in-rag--push-the-defense-earlier-than-the-prompt].
