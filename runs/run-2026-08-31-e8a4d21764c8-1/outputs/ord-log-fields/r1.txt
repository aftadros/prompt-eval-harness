On every model call, log enough to reconstruct the call:

- **Request ID**
- **Model and version**
- **Input and output token counts**
- **Latency**, split into:
  - Time-to-first-token
  - Total latency
- **Which retry attempt it was**
- **Whether the circuit breaker was open**
- **Which path served the response**: model, fallback, or kill switch

Also, avoid logging secrets. Treat prompts and user content as sensitive and handle them accordingly [llm-api-from-php-in-production--log-enough-to-reconstruct-one-call].
