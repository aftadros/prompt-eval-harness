For every model call, log:

- **Request ID**
- **Model and version**
- **Input token count**
- **Output token count**
- **Latency**, split into:
  - Time-to-first-token
  - Total latency
- **Retry attempt number**
- **Whether the circuit breaker was open**
- **Which path served the response**: model, fallback, or kill switch

Also, avoid logging secrets and treat prompts/user content as sensitive rather than freely logging them [llm-api-from-php-in-production--log-enough-to-reconstruct-one-call].
