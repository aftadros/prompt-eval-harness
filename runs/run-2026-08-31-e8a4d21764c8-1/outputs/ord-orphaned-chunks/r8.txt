An **orphaned chunk** is “a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten months ago, still sitting in the index” [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because it can keep being retrieved and “winning retrieval for some unlucky query,” which means users may get stale guidance even though the source document has changed or disappeared [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. In other words, a RAG system that only adds new chunks without deleting old ones will preserve outdated fragments forever and can make answers look wrong or out of date [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
