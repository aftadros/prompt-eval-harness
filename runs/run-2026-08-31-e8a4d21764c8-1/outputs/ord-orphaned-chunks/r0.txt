An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but whose vectors remain in the retrieval index. It can still be retrieved and “win” for some query even though it no longer represents the current source material [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause **stale answers**. If a policy changes, hours are updated, or a deprecated procedure is removed, an old chunk can keep surfacing and mislead users—sometimes without anyone noticing until someone acts on outdated guidance [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds new chunks preserves these stale fragments indefinitely, so deletes and reconciliation are just as important as writes [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
