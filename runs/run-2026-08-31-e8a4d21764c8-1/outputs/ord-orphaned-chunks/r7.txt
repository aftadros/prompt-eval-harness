An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a source document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vector remains in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because it can keep winning retrieval for queries even though it no longer reflects the current document. The result is stale or incorrect guidance: users may act on outdated information before anyone notices that the assistant’s index has drifted from reality [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
