An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten earlier, but whose vectors are still in the index and can win retrieval for some query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance without realizing it, and a pipeline that only adds new chunks keeps those orphans around forever [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. That’s why deletes matter as much as writes—retrieval shouldn’t straddle old and new versions of the same document [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
