An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a source document that was removed or rewritten, but whose old vectors are still left in the index and can win retrieval for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: an assistant may surface guidance from an outdated version of a document, and users might act on that stale information before anyone notices the problem [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. If your indexing pipeline only adds new chunks without deleting old ones, it keeps every orphaned chunk forever [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. That’s why deletes matter just as much as writes, and why you should also run reconciliation to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
