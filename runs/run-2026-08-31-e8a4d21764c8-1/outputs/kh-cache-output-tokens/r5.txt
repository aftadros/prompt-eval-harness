No—not directly. Prompt caching lowers the cost of the **input** portion by billing a stable prefix at a cache rate instead of full input price [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--caching-the-stable-prefix]. Output tokens are priced separately and still charged per token, typically at a higher rate than input [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--tokens-are-the-row-count].

So caching can cut your effective **input cost** and overall request cost, but it does not by itself reduce the cost of generated output.
