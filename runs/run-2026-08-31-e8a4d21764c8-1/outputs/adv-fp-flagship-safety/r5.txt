No—the documents do not say that safety-critical answers should *always* go to the flagship model. They argue against defaulting everything to the most expensive model and instead recommend routing by question difficulty, retrieval confidence, or escalation when a check flags an answer as thin [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--which-model-gets-the-question].

On guardrails specifically, they say each safety layer has a cost and should be chosen deliberately: some checks can run on every request, others only on samples, and you shouldn’t use a heavy verification pass unless the risk justifies it [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--guardrails-have-a-cost-too]. So the posts support using stronger models or extra checks when warranted, not an unconditional “always flagship” rule for safety-critical answers.
