Split your golden set into three labeled buckets [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set]:

1. **Ordinary questions** – the everyday queries that should just work.
2. **Known-hard cases** – questions that exposed a bug at some point and need to stay guarded.
3. **Adversarial cases** – inputs designed to try pulling the model off its guardrails (e.g., leaking restricted content or forcing a hallucinated policy).

The same document notes that fifty good cases across these buckets beat five hundred lazy ones, and every case must be able to fail or it's just decoration [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set].
