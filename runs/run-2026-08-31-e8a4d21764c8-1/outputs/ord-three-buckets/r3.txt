Keep **three labeled buckets**:

1. **Ordinary questions** – everyday queries that should just work.
2. **Known-hard cases** – questions that exposed a bug once and need ongoing coverage.
3. **Adversarial cases** – inputs designed to pull the model off its guardrails.

The document notes that "fifty good cases beat five hundred lazy ones, and every case has to be able to fail or it's decoration" [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set].
