He doesn’t recommend one specific numeric chunk size. His approach is to chunk along the document’s own structure—headings, list items with their parents, and tables with captions—and use a token limit only as a fallback when a section is too long. The 512-token window mentioned in the source is an example of fixed-window chunking, not his recommended size [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find].
