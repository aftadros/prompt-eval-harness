An orphaned chunk is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but whose old vectors are still in the index and may “win retrieval” for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. You should care because they cause users to act on stale guidance, often without anyone noticing until it becomes a real problem; a pipeline that only adds chunks keeps them forever unless deletes or reconciliation jobs remove old versions and chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
