An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its vectors are still sitting in the index and can win retrieval for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. You should care because they produce stale answers—possibly from guidance that no longer applies—and if your pipeline only adds new chunks without deleting old ones, every orphaned chunk stays around forever [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
