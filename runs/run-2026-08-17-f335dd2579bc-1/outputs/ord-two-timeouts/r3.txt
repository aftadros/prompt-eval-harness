You set two timeouts because an LLM call has two different failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to fail quickly, or it may be reachable but slow while generating a real answer, so you’re willing to wait longer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. A single timeout would force one behavior for both situations—either giving up too early on slow generation or waiting too long during an outage. In the example, `connect_timeout` is 2 seconds and total `timeout` is 30 seconds; with streaming, the total-timeout logic changes because you’re bounding idle gaps between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
