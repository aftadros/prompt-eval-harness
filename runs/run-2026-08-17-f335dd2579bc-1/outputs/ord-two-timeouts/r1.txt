You set two different timeouts because an LLM API call can fail in two very different ways: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to give up quickly; or it may be reachable but slow to finish generating, so you’re willing to wait longer for a real answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

In the example, `connect_timeout` is the fast “outage tripwire,” while `timeout` is the longer ceiling for waiting on generation [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With streaming, that total timeout changes meaning because the connection stays open while tokens arrive, so you end up bounding the idle gap between chunks instead of the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
