An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but whose old vectors are still in the index and can win retrieval for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: an outdated piece of guidance can keep showing up as if it were current. If your indexing pipeline only adds chunks instead of deleting old versions, orphaned chunks can persist indefinitely and “rot” assistant responses [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
