An **orphaned chunk** is “a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten months ago” that is still present in the index and can keep winning retrieval for queries, even though its original content may be stale or gone [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they are described as “the classic production failure” in this RAG context: a pipeline that only adds chunks without deleting old ones can keep every orphaned chunk forever, causing answers to rely on outdated guidance. The recommended fix is to delete old vectors when documents change and run reconciliation jobs to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
