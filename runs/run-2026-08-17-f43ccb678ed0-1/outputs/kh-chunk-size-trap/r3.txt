He doesn’t recommend one fixed numeric chunk size. Instead, he recommends chunking along the document’s own structure—splitting on headings and keeping related items together—and using a token limit only as a fallback when a section is genuinely too long [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find]. The 512-token window mentioned in the source is an example of fixed-window chunking, not his recommended size.
