He does not recommend a single universal chunk size. Instead, he recommends chunking along each document’s own structure first—splitting on headings and keeping lists and tables with their parent context—and using the token limit only as a fallback when a section is too long [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find]. The 512-token window he mentions is an example of fixed-window chunking that can create bad seams, not his recommended size.
