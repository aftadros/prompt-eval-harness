An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but whose old vectors are still left in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they can keep winning retrieval for queries even though the source content no longer exists or is outdated. That causes assistants to return stale guidance, and users may act on it without realizing the answer is out of date [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. In a pipeline that only adds new chunks and never deletes old ones, these orphaned fragments can persist indefinitely [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
