An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors are still sitting in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. You should care because it can win retrieval for an unlucky query and cause users to act on stale guidance; if your indexing pipeline only adds new chunks and never deletes superseded ones, those orphaned fragments persist forever [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
