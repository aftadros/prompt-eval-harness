An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors remain in the index and can still win retrieval for some query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: if your assistant retrieves an orphaned chunk, users may act on outdated guidance without anyone noticing until something goes wrong [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds chunks keeps these orphans forever, so you need to delete old vectors when documents change and run reconciliation jobs to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
