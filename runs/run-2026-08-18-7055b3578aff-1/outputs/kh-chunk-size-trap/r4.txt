He does not give a specific recommended chunk size. Instead, he recommends chunking along the document’s own structure—splitting on headings, keeping list items with their parent sections, and keeping tables with captions—and using a token limit only as a fallback when a section is too long. The 512-token window mentioned is an example of fixed-window chunking that can fail on heterogeneous internal docs [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find].
