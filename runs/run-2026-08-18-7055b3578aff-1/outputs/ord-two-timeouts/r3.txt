You use two timeouts because an LLM API call can fail in two different ways: the provider may be unreachable, or it may be reachable but taking a long time to generate the answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. A short connect timeout lets you fail fast on an outage, while a longer total timeout lets a slow-but-valid generation finish [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. For streaming calls, the second timeout can instead bound the idle gap between chunks rather than the whole call [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
