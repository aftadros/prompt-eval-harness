An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors remain in the index and can still win retrieval for some query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance without realizing it. A pipeline that only adds new data keeps every orphan forever, so deletes are just as important as writes to keep retrieval accurate and prevent old document versions from mixing with current ones [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
