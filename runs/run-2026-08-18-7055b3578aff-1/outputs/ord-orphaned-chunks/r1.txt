An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its vectors are still sitting in the index and can win retrieval for queries [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance from content that no longer exists or is no longer current. A pipeline that only adds new chunks without deleting old ones will keep every orphaned chunk forever, so the assistant can keep surfacing obsolete information [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
