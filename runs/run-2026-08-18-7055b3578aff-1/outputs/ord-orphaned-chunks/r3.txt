An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its vectors remain in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. You should care because it is described as “the classic production failure” for RAG freshness: an orphaned chunk can keep winning retrieval and cause users to act on stale guidance, even though nobody may notice until a bad answer surfaces [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds new chunks without deleting old ones will preserve those orphans indefinitely [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
