An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten long ago, but its vectors are still in the index and can win retrieval for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance before anyone notices, especially if the indexing pipeline only adds new chunks and never deletes old ones [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. To prevent this, delete a document’s old vectors when writing its replacement, and run reconciliation to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
