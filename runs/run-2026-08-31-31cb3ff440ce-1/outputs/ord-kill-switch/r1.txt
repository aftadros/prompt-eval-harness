In practice, a kill switch is a coarse, boring config flag for an LLM feature that can turn it off without a deploy and is read on every request, with no cache delaying its effect [shipping-llm-features-safely--the-kill-switch]. When turned off, the feature drops to its fallback or graceful-degradation path instead of becoming an error state: for example, the assistant says the summarized answer is unavailable while retrieval and the rest of the app keep working [shipping-llm-features-safely--the-kill-switch]. It is meant to be a known, tested mode that can be rehearsed and triggered within seconds during incidents such as a bad prompt change, unsafe provider output, a cost spike from a runaway loop, or a guardrail regression [shipping-llm-features-safely--the-kill-switch].
