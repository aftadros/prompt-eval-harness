The documents don’t say he recommends disabling retries entirely. They recommend keeping retries narrow and bounded: retry only transient failures—connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—with a hard cap, backoff, jitter, and respect for `Retry-After` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

Everything else should fail immediately: malformed requests, auth errors, context-length overflow, or a 200 response with unusable output are not retry candidates because retrying them is unlikely to change the result and may waste time, money, or add load to an already struggling service [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
