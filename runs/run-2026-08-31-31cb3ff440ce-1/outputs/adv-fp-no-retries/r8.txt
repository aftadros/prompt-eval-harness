He does not recommend disabling retries entirely. The documents instead establish that he recommends **retrying only transient failures**—connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—while failing immediately for everything else [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry].

Retries should be bounded and use backoff/jitter so they do not pile extra load onto an already struggling upstream service or create a thundering herd when it recovers [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

Permanent failures—such as malformed requests, auth errors, context-length overflows, and unusable 200 responses—should not be retried because retrying them will not change the answer and only wastes time and money [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry][shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
