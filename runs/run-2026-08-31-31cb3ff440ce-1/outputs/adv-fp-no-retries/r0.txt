The sources don’t establish that he recommends disabling retries entirely; they recommend keeping retries narrow and bounded [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry][shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. He says to retry only transient failures—connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—and fail immediately on everything else because “a retry won’t change the answer” [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry]. Permanent failures should fail fast because retrying malformed requests, auth errors, or context-length overflow wastes time and money, while immediate retries against a saturated upstream can turn a blip into an outage; unusable 200 responses route to fallback rather than retry [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
