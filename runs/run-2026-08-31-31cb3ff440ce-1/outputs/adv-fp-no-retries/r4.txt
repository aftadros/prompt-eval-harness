He does **not** recommend disabling retries entirely. He recommends restricting them to genuinely transient failures—connection errors, timeouts, 429s, and 5xx responses—and failing fast on everything else [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry].

His reasoning is that retrying non-transient problems is wasteful or harmful: a malformed request will just return the same error, an uncapped retry loop can turn a slow provider into your own outage and extra bill, and hammering an already saturated service with immediate retries can make a brief blip worse [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. So the docs establish bounded, backoff-based retries for transient issues, not total retry disabling.
