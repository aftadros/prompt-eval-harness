The documents do **not** show him recommending disabling retries entirely. They establish a narrower rule: retry only genuinely transient failures—such as connection errors, timeouts, 429 rate limits, and 5xx responses—and fail immediately on permanent failures [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].

Retries should be bounded, with backoff and jitter so they don’t pile load onto an already struggling service [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry] [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger]. A successful 200 response containing unusable output is not retried by default; it goes to a fallback path instead [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
