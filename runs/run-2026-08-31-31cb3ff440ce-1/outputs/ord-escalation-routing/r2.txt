The pattern is to **classify the request, then route it by difficulty and confidence**:

- **Cheap tier:** short factual lookups with a clean retrieval hit, where the model mostly reformats retrieved text.  
- **Smart/expensive tier:** ambiguous, multi-step, synthesis-heavy questions, or cases that span multiple documents, require reasoning over conflicting sources, or came back from retrieval weakly.  

The classifier can be the small model itself or even a cheap rules check on retrieval confidence [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--which-model-gets-the-question].

A closely related variant is **escalation**: start with the cheap model, then if a confidence check or guardrail flags the answer as thin, retry once on the expensive model [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--which-model-gets-the-question].
