An orphaned chunk is “a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten months ago” that remains in the RAG index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. You should care because it can “win retrieval for some unlucky query,” causing the assistant to return stale guidance and potentially leading users to act on outdated information [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
