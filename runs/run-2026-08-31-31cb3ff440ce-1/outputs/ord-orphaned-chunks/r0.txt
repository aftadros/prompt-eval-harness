An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors remain in the index and can still win retrieval for a query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: if an assistant retrieves an orphaned chunk, users may act on outdated guidance without anyone noticing until it becomes a problem [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds chunks keeps every one of these forever, so you need deletion behavior—removing old vectors when documents change and reconciling away chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
