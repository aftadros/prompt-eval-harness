An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten long ago but still remains in the index [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because it can keep winning retrieval for queries and cause the assistant to return stale guidance, even though the underlying source has changed or disappeared [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds new chunks preserves these stale fragments forever, so deletes are just as important as writes [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
