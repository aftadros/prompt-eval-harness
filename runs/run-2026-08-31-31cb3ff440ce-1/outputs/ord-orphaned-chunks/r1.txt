An **orphaned chunk** is “a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten months ago, still sitting in the index and winning retrieval for some unlucky query” [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they are a classic production failure mode: stale chunks can keep being retrieved after their source document is gone or changed, causing users to act on outdated guidance. A pipeline that only adds vectors “keeps every one of those forever” [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
