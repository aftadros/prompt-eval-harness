An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten earlier, but its vectors are still sitting in the index and can “win retrieval” for an unlucky query [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: if your pipeline only adds new chunks and never deletes old ones, those outdated fragments stay around forever and users may act on guidance that no longer applies [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. The fix is to delete a document’s old vectors when it changes—ideally in the same operation/transaction as writing new ones—and run reconciliation jobs to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
