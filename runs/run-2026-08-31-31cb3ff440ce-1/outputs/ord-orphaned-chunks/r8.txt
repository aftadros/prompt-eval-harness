An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that was removed or rewritten, but its old vectors are still sitting in the index and can “win retrieval for some unlucky query” [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause stale answers: if an index keeps old versions around, a user may act on outdated guidance after a document has changed or been deleted [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem]. A pipeline that only adds new vectors “keeps every one of those forever,” so deletes and reconciliation matter as much as writes to prevent retrieval from straddling two versions of a document [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
