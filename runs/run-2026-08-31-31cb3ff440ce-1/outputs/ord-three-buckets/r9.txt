A golden set should be split into three labeled buckets [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set]:

1. **Ordinary questions** — everyday queries that should just work.
2. **Known-hard questions** — cases that exposed a bug once and need to keep failing loudly if the regression returns.
3. **Adversarial questions** — inputs designed to pull the model off its guardrails (e.g., tricking it into inventing policy or leaking restricted content).

The guidance also notes that fifty good cases across these buckets beat five hundred lazy ones, and every case must be able to fail or it's just decoration [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set].
