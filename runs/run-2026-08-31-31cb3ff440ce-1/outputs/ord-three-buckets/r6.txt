You should split your golden set into **three labeled buckets**:

1. **Ordinary questions** – ones that should just work.
2. **Known-hard ones** – cases that exposed a bug once.
3. **Adversarial ones** – cases that try to pull the model off its guardrails.

The document notes that fifty good cases across these buckets beat five hundred lazy ones, and every case has to be able to fail or it's decoration [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set].
