Indirect prompt injection is when an instruction planted in content the retrieval system fetches—such as an indexed document, wiki page, or email—is read by the model and followed [prompt-injection-in-rag--opening].

It differs from direct prompt injection because the direct kind comes openly from a user typing something like “ignore your rules” into the chat, while the indirect kind rides in on retrieved material from a source the user may never see and an admin may have forgotten was indexed [prompt-injection-in-rag--opening].
