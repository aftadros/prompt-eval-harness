The single most important defense is **enforcing permissions at retrieval time**, so documents the user isn’t allowed to see are filtered out before they’re ranked or placed in the prompt [prompt-injection-in-rag--push-the-defense-earlier-than-the-prompt].

That permission filter is “the load-bearing piece,” because even if a planted instruction succeeds, it still can’t reach data the user was never cleared for; other mitigations mostly just make attacks more expensive [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
