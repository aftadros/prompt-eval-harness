No. Retrieved chunks from different sources should not be flattened into a single undifferentiated block of context; they should carry their provenance so lower-trust sources are treated with more suspicion than vetted or access-restricted ones [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--retrieved-content-is-hostile-input].
