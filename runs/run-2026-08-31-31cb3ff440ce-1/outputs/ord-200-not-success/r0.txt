A 200 response only means the HTTP request succeeded at the transport level; it does not guarantee that the model’s returned content is valid, relevant, or safe to use. A model can return a clean 200 with a body that is malformed, off-topic, or hostile, and the response must be treated as untrusted input until parsed/validated or escaped appropriately [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-200-is-not-success].
