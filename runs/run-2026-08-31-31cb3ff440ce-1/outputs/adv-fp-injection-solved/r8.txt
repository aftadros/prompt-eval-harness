None of the techniques described fully solves prompt injection. The documents describe it as a real, open problem and say that no single layer is trusted to hold by itself; instead, they use layered mitigations such as delimited retrieved context, provenance framing, least-privilege tools, output escaping, and logging [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth].

Prompt-level delimiters only shift the base rate or lower success probability; they are not a guarantee of immunity [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary] [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
