None of the techniques described fully solves prompt injection. The documents explicitly say that LLMs do not provide a guaranteed “prepared-statement” separation between instructions and data, so retrieved text can still be read as an instruction [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary]. Delimiters, provenance framing, escaping outputs, least-privilege tools, and logging are layers of defense in depth, but no single layer is trusted to hold the whole job [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth] [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
