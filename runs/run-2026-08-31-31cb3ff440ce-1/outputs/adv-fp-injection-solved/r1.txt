The documents explicitly say that LLMs lack a prepared-statement-style separation between instructions and data, so retrieved text can still be read as an instruction [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary]. Delimited reference blocks are not immunity; they only shift the base rate and give you a place to reason about failures [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary]. The broader defense-in-depth approach is used precisely because “no single layer gets trusted with the whole job” [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth]. In RAG, output escaping, tool scoping, and permission filtering reduce risk and raise attacker cost, but they do not make the model safe to point at arbitrary text [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output].
