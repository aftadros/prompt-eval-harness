None of the techniques described here fully solves prompt injection. The documents say LLMs lack a prepared-statement-style separation between instructions and untrusted data [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary], that delimiters and framing only shift the base rate rather than provide immunity [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--the-data-instruction-boundary], and that prompt injection remains “a real, open problem” where no single layer gets trusted with the whole job [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth]. The RAG-specific controls—marked context regions, escaping output, least-privilege tools, provenance checks, logging—are mitigations meant to make a successful injection less useful or less likely, not a complete solution [prompt-injection-in-rag--the-prompt-and-the-output] [prompt-injection-is-untrusted-input--defense-in-depth].
