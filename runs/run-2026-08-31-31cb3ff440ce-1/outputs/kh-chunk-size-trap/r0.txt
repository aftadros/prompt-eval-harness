He doesn’t recommend a specific numeric chunk size. Instead, he recommends chunking along the document’s own structure first—splitting on headings, keeping list items with their parents, and keeping tables with captions—and using a token limit only as a fallback when a single section is genuinely too long [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find]. The 512-token window mentioned is an example of the fixed-window approach he criticizes, not his recommendation.
