They recommend a small bounded ceiling—effectively **about two retries**, meaning an initial model call plus up to **two retry attempts** (three total requests). The PHP example enforces a hard cap with `if ($attempt >= 3) return false;` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry], and the other post says retries are “bounded, usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
