The posts recommend **bounded retries, usually around two retry attempts**—not unlimited retrying. The PHP example enforces a hard cap by stopping when `$attempt >= 3` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry], and the other post explicitly says retries are “bounded, usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
