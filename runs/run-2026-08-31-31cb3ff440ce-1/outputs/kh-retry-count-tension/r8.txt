They recommend a small fixed limit—typically **two retries after the initial call**, i.e., no more than three total calls for transient failures.

The PHP example stops retrying once `$attempt >= 3` [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry], and the other post says retries are “bounded, usually at two” [shipping-llm-features-safely--retries-that-dont-make-the-fire-bigger].
