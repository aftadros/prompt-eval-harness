You set two different timeouts because an LLM API call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, where you want to fail quickly in a second or two, or it may be reachable but slow while generating a real answer, where you’re willing to wait longer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. Separating them lets you use a short connect timeout as an outage tripwire and a longer total timeout for generation completion [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
