You set two different timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to fail quickly after a second or two, or it may be reachable but slow to finish generating, in which case you’re willing to wait longer for the actual answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

In the Guzzle example, `connect_timeout => 2` is the “outage tripwire,” while `timeout => 30` is the total ceiling for a non-streaming call waiting for generation to complete [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
