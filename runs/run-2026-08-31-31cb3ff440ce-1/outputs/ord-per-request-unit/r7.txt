Track cost per request because it gives you an immediately actionable signal, whereas the monthly bill lags and averages everything together. A per-request number shows right away whether a change moved costs, survives growth (traffic doubling can just double the total without revealing inefficiency), and lets you pinpoint the cause—such as an expensive feature, a bloated prompt at one endpoint, or a tenant overusing the flagship model [llm-cost-per-request--why-the-unit-is-the-request]. The monthly bill “averages everything together and hides the offender,” while per-request costs bucketed by feature show whether a change actually helped [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--tokens-are-the-row-count].
