They recommend routing by query difficulty: simple, high-confidence factual lookups can use the cheap model; harder multi-document or weak-retrieval questions go to the smart model; and escalation lets you start cheaply and retry on the smart model if a confidence check or guardrail flags the answer as thin [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--which-model-gets-the-question].

For safety layers, they say each check has a cost and should have a known price: some checks may run on every request, others only on a sample, and you shouldn’t spend a heavy verification pass protecting a short answer unless the risk warrants it [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--guardrails-have-a-cost-too].
