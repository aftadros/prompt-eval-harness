You missed a **deadline around the whole retried operation**, separate from the per-call timeout. A per-call timeout only bounds one attempt; three attempts against a thirty-second timeout can produce ninety seconds plus backoff before your fallback fires, leaving the user waiting far too long [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation]. Put an overall time budget on the operation and stop retrying/degrade when that deadline passes.
