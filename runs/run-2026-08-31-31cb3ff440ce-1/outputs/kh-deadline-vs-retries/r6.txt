You missed an **overall deadline for the whole operation**, separate from each individual call’s timeout.

With retries, each attempt can still run to its full per-call limit—so three attempts against a thirty-second timeout can become ninety seconds plus backoff before your fallback fires. That makes the worst-case user wait much longer, even though retries improve the odds for an individual request. The fix is to bound the total time the operation may take and stop retrying/degrade when that deadline passes [llm-api-from-php-in-production--a-deadline-on-the-whole-operation].
