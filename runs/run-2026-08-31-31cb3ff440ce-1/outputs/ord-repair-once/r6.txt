Give the model **one repair attempt** after the first malformed-JSON failure—so that’s **two attempts total**. Feed it the specific parse or validation error and ask again; if the second answer is still bad, stop retrying and fall back to whatever your feature can do without the extracted data [llm-json-in-php--one-repair-attempt].

If you mean transient API failures rather than malformed output—connection errors, timeouts, 429s, or 5xx responses—the documented rule is a hard cap of **3 attempts** [llm-api-from-php-in-production--which-failures-to-retry].
