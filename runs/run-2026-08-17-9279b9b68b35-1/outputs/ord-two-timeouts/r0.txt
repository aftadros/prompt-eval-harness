You use two timeouts because an LLM call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, in which case you should fail quickly after a second or two; or it may be reachable but slow to finish generating, where waiting longer is reasonable because a real answer is still coming [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

A single timeout can’t separate those cases well. The shorter connect timeout acts as an outage tripwire, while the longer total timeout gives generation time to complete [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].
