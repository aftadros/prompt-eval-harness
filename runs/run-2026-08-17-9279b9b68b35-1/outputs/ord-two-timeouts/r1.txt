Because an LLM call has two different failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, so you want to fail fast; or it may be reachable but still generating slowly, in which case a real answer is coming and you can wait longer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With one timeout, you’d either cut off slow-but-legitimate generations too early or hang too long on an unreachable provider.
