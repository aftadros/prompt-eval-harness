You set two timeouts because an LLM API call has two distinct failure modes: the provider may be unreachable, or it may be reachable but slow to generate [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts].

A short **connect timeout** lets you fail fast when you can’t reach the service at all, while a longer **total response timeout** gives a reachable model enough time to finish generating an answer [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts]. With streaming, the second timeout is better used as an idle-gap limit between chunks, so a slow but visibly progressing response can stay open instead of being failed and retried [llm-api-from-php-in-production--two-timeouts][llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
