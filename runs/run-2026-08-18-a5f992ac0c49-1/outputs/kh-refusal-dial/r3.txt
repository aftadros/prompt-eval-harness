No—not unconditionally. Refusal is a tradeoff: if the assistant is “too cautious,” it will “shrug at questions it could have answered,” which can train people to stop asking [rag-over-internal-docs--the-honest-i-dont-know]. But for documentation people rely on to make decisions, the docs recommend leaning toward refusing when unsure, because a confident wrong answer may be acted on and discovered later, whereas “I couldn’t find that in the docs” only costs someone a trip to a human [rag-over-internal-docs--the-honest-i-dont-know]. In short: refuse when uncertainty is costly or retrieval looks weak, but avoid over-refusal so the assistant remains useful.
