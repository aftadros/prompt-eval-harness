He doesn’t recommend a single fixed chunk size for internal docs. Instead, he says to “chunk along the document’s own structure first,” splitting on headings and keeping list items with their parent, while letting the token limit act only as “a fallback for when a single section is genuinely too long” [rag-over-internal-docs--chunking-decides-what-retrieval-can-find]. The 512-token window he mentions is an example of a fixed-window approach that can create bad seams, not a recommended size.
