According to the golden set guidance, you should split your cases into **three labeled buckets**:

1. **Ordinary questions** that should just work
2. **Known-hard ones** that exposed a bug once
3. **Adversarial ones** that try to pull the model off its guardrails

The document also notes that "fifty good cases beat five hundred lazy ones, and every case has to be able to fail or it's decoration" [evals-before-vibes--the-golden-set].
