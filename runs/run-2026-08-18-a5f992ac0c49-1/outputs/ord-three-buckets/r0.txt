The golden set should be split into **three labeled buckets**:

1. **Ordinary questions** — cases that should just work under normal conditions.
2. **Known-hard ones** — questions that exposed a bug once, so they serve as regression guards.
3. **Adversarial ones** — inputs designed to try to pull the model off its guardrails.
