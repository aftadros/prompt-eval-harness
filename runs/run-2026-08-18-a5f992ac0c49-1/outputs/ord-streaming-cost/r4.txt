No—not directly. Streaming does not make the model cheaper or faster to generate; you still pay for the same output tokens and the model spends the same time producing them [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].

It can reduce overall cost indirectly by improving perceived latency: users are less likely to retry a long blank response, avoiding paying for duplicate generations [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries]. It also changes timeout behavior so an in-progress answer can keep the connection open instead of failing and triggering another paid call [llm-cost-at-campus-scale--streaming-and-retries].
