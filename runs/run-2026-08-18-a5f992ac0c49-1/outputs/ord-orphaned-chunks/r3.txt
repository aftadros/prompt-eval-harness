An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but its old vectors remain in the index and can still be retrieved for queries [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because they cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance without realizing it. A pipeline that only adds new chunks keeps these orphaned fragments forever, so deletion is just as important as writing updated vectors [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
