An **orphaned chunk** is a fragment of a document that has been removed or rewritten, but whose vectors are still left in the index. It can keep winning retrieval for queries even though it no longer reflects any current source document [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].

You should care because orphaned chunks cause stale answers: users may act on outdated guidance without realizing it, and a pipeline that only adds new vectors will preserve those bad fragments forever. That’s why the recommended approach is to delete old vectors when re-indexing changed documents and run reconciliation jobs to remove chunks whose source document no longer exists [rag-over-internal-docs--freshness-is-an-indexing-problem].
